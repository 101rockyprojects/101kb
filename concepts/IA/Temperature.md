## What Is Temperature in AI?

Temperature controls the **randomness** of word selection in AI-generated responses. When the model predicts the next word, it assigns probabilities to different possible choices. A **low temperature** setting makes the model choose the **most likely** word, while a **high temperature** setting encourages **less predictable, more diverse responses**.
This is very important in *LLMs* but can be set on *agents* to improve workflow dynamic.

## How Temperature Works

- **Low Temperature (0.1–0.3):** The AI prioritizes high-probability words, resulting in **fact-based, deterministic responses**.
- **Medium Temperature (0.4–0.7):** A balance between structured logic and creativity, leading to **coherent yet engaging responses**.
- **High Temperature (0.8–1.5):** AI explores lower-probability words, leading to **highly varied, imaginative, and unexpected responses**.
